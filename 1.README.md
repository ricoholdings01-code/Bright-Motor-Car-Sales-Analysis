# 🚗 Bright Motors Car Sales Analysis

## 📌 Overview

* This project focuses on analyzing historical car sales and pricing data from **Bright Motors** to generate business insights for the newly appointed Head of Sales.
* The analysis helps the business understand:

  * Revenue performance
  * Customer purchasing trends
  * Regional sales distribution
  * Vehicle profitability
  * Inventory optimization opportunities
* The project demonstrates end-to-end data analytics workflow including:

  * Data cleaning
  * SQL transformation
  * Dashboard development
  * Business intelligence reporting
  * Executive presentation design 

---

# ❗ Problem Statement

* Bright Motors needs to improve dealership profitability, optimize inventory, and expand sales performance across regions.
* The company currently lacks:

  * Clear visibility into high-performing vehicle models
  * Understanding of pricing and profitability trends
  * Insights into customer purchasing behavior
  * Regional sales performance tracking
* The new Head of Sales requires actionable insights to support strategic business decisions and future growth. 

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze car sales transactional data
* Identify top-performing car makes and models
* Understand relationships between:

  * Selling price
  * Mileage
  * Manufacturing year
* Evaluate regional sales performance
* Detect emerging customer purchasing trends
* Build dashboards and visual reports
* Deliver strategic business recommendations
